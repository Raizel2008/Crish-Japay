Information Details
Information Time 10minutes

Total Time 25minutes
Servings 4 people

Homemade Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Prepare a large mixing bowl.
  2. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Add the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  4. Mix all ingredients until combined.
  5. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  6. Pour some pancake batter into the pan.
  7. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  8. Flip the pancake and cook the other side.
  9. Repeat until all the batter is used.
  10. Serve with syrup or your favorite toppings.

Preparation Information

  • Preparation Time: 10 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 4 people
  • Difficulty: Easy
